Privacy Policy — HK Smart Parking Finder

Last updated: 18 September 2026

HK Smart Parking Finder ("the app") is a simple tool that shows live Hong Kong car park vacancy information. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers operated by the developer that any of your data passes through.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to sort nearby car parks by distance and to show you how far away each one is. This location is used only inside the app, on your own device, for that single purpose. It is never sent anywhere, never stored, and never shared with the developer or any third party. If you decline location access, the app still works fully — you can search and browse by district or name instead.

Live parking data

The app fetches live, publicly available car park vacancy data directly from the Hong Kong Government's official open data services:

  • Transport Department — Parking Vacancy Data (One-Stop Version), via api.data.gov.hk
  • Transport Department — territory-wide car park vacancy feed, via resource.data.one.gov.hk

These requests ask only for public parking data and contain no personal information about you. They are governed by the Hong Kong Government's own open data terms, available at data.gov.hk.

No accounts, no storage

The app does not ask you to sign in, does not create an account for you, and does not store any information about you or your device — either on the device or on any server — beyond what your browser or device already keeps for its own normal operation (such as standard app cache), which the developer cannot access.

Third parties

Other than the two Hong Kong Government data feeds listed above, and standard Android/Google Play services required to run any Android app, the app does not connect to or share data with any third-party service, advertiser, or analytics provider.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
