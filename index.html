About this tool & data sources

This finder calls two official Hong Kong government feeds directly from your browser, refreshed automatically every 60 seconds — nothing passes through any server of mine. For Private car and Motorcycle, it combines the Transport Department's territory-wide vacancy feed (all 18 districts) with the Parking Vacancy Data (One-Stop Version) API. For LGV/HGV, only the one-stop API has a vehicle type actually stated, so those two views are limited to the car parks it covers — which, in practice, means Kwun Tong, Kowloon Bay, San Po Kong and Kai Tak (the Energizing Kowloon East smart-parking zone). That gap is itself a data point: official real-time reporting for goods vehicles barely exists outside one pilot district, even though HK logged 2M+ illegal-parking tickets last year and the shortage is most acute for goods vehicles — which is exactly the underserved segment the original NYC analysis pointed toward.

Vacancy figures come from car park operators and are not available for every venue — where an operator reports a status only (not an exact count), or reports nothing at all, this is shown as "Status only" or "No live data" rather than a number. Distances are straight-line, calculated from the location your browser reports.

Built as a follow-on to an MBA data analytics project on NYC curb-parking enforcement — testing whether the same "smart parking" idea holds up in a market that, unlike NYC, already has strong public real-time parking data. Short answer: general car-park search is well served here already; the sharper open gap is commercial/goods-vehicle loading, which is why LGV/HGV views are included above.
